What the report says about education

How education uses AI

Mostly for content creation, summarising papers and notes, curriculum planning and administration, rather than direct teaching.

The main concern

Keeping academic integrity intact while using AI to support learning.

What matters most

AI literacy as a foundation, critical evaluation of outputs, and knowing when AI is appropriate and when human oversight must stay.
